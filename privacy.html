Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Plushtones collects no data. Nothing leaves your device.

Microphone

When your child holds an animal, the app records a short sound so the animal can sing it back. The sound stays in memory and is gone when the app closes. It is never saved or sent.

iOS asks for the microphone at the first start.

You can turn the microphone off in Settings › Plushtones.

No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet.

Children

Plushtones is for children aged 5 and under. We collect nothing from them.

Apple

If you share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Mikrofon

Abschalten kannst du das Mikrofon unter Einstellungen › Plushtones.
